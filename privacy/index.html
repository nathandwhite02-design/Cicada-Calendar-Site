Cicada Calendar Privacy Policy

Cicada is a private household calendar hub. This policy explains how calendar information is used when you choose to connect a calendar service.

Google Calendar access

Cicada uses access to Google Calendar only to provide the calendar synchronisation functionality you request. Calendar data is used to display, create, update and delete calendar events as directed by you.

How your data is handled

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with advertisers. Authentication credentials and access tokens are used only to maintain the calendar connection you requested.

Your choices

You can disconnect a calendar from Cicada at any time. You can also revoke Cicada's access to Google Calendar through the permissions settings in your Google Account.

This public website

This public website uses no analytics, advertising cookies, or tracking. It contains no forms, advertising, external scripts, or third-party fonts.
